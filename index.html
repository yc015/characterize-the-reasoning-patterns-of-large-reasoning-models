How LOT works

Inductive taxonomy construction for long-form thoughts

LOT borrows from qualitative research: compare two traces, name the distinguishing behaviors, annotate new data, and expand the taxonomy whenever classification fails.

Step 01

Compare paired reasonings

LLM annotators read a pair of traces produced by two LRMs on the same prompt and verbalize distinguishing thought patterns鈥攅.g., 鈥渧erifies hypotheses before committing鈥?or 鈥渓oops through identical evidence.鈥?/p>

LLM proposes distinguishing reasoning traits (e.g., hypothesis switches) by comparing two models on the same problem.

Encoding choices

PoR records whether a behavior appears. BoR counts how often it surfaces per trace, capturing habits such as repeated self-checks or code-simulation loops that matter on longer benchmarks.

Training signal

A logistic classifier maps annotated vectors to their source LRM. When it fails, LOT requests new features for the misclassified traces, keeping the taxonomy open-ended but grounded in data.

Scaling & discipline

Larger Qwen3 variants reason with tighter control

LOT separates Qwen3-32B from distilled variants with 80鈥?3% accuracy on GPQA. Larger models consistently keep track of constraints, verify theories, and avoid circular evidence loops.

  • BoR encodings add 3鈥?4% accuracy over PoR by tracking how frequently a behavior appears.
  • Smaller models re-evaluate the same fact, misapply scientific laws, and switch concepts mid-solution.

Base fingerprints

Family-level priors shape reasoning cadence

Models that share a base (e.g., Qwen derivatives) are harder to distinguish, while cross-family pairs show sharper contrasts in verification rituals and problem decomposition.

  • For long traces, PoR saturates but BoR still separates Phi-4-RP, Magistral, and Nemotron descendants.
  • Qwen3-14B simulates execution on LiveCodeBench, whereas QwQ-32B inspects parameters and recursion depth.

Domain inertia

Seed-Coder brings coding reflexes to math

Although tuned on coding data, Seed-Coder-8B-Reasoning tackles 20% of MATH-500 problems by writing and simulating Python functions, compared to 2% for Qwen3-8B.

  • LOT exposes when specialized fine-tuning 鈥渟ticks,鈥?even off-distribution.
  • Such inertia can overfit solution styles and leave accuracy on the table.
